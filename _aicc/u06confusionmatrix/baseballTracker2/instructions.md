



The Design Thinking process involves multiple steps... empathize, define, ideate, prototype, build, and test

In Part 2, you will Ideate, Prototype, and Test

Ideate: It is time to be creative. All ideas should be considered. Ideas can address one or more of the core problems. Even an idea that results in a part of the solution is useful.

Prototype: One idea is selected to be developed through mock-ups, storyboards, staging, etc. The objective is to try things out and find the best solutions for the core problems based on the initial ideas.

Test: Test the final product against the defined problem and collect feedback from the product owner and/or end users.

```
Step 1: (Ideate) Describe/pitch a several different ways that an app could work. What other types of input could be used? What other types of output could be generated?

Step 2: (Prototype) Pick your simplest solution. Draw a simple flowchart diagram (no more than 10 steps) that shows how a user will interact with the app.

Step 3: (Build & Test) Code a very simple beta version (v0.01) of the app. This version only needs the most basic features.
```
